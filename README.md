
# Hydroinformatics — Oil & Gas Services Website

A business website showcasing professional services in the oil and gas industry, including subsea construction, navigation aids, hydrographic surveys, and engineering support.

## 🌐 About the Project

The website presents services offered across the oil and gas and marine engineering sectors, helping visitors learn about the company's capabilities and professional services.

## 🛠️ Services Showcased

- Geophysical Seabed Survey
- Bathymetric Survey
- Geotechnical Engineering
- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Rig Move and Rig Positioning
- Pipeline Route Survey
- Dredge Monitoring and Supervision
- Topo-Geodetic Survey
- Cable Route Survey
- Seismic Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC)
- Hydrographic Survey
- Engineering support, including pipeline and platform installations

## 💻 Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- PHP
- MySQL

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- Complete business website for an oil and gas services company.
- Frontend development using HTML and CSS.
- Backend functionality using PHP.
- Database integration using MySQL.
